Feedback failure cases to verify before implementation:
- Header action hidden in web or moved out of desktop chrome; mismatched button height; narrow viewport overflow.
- Wrong active tool, unavailable tool options, general feedback outside a tool.
- Empty/whitespace/overlong notes; double submit; loss of draft after errors or dismissal.
- Keyboard escape, focus trapping/restoration, inaccessible fields or status.
- Stored feedback lost when email fails; false claim of notification success; forged notification recipient.
- Invalid payload or rate limit sends an email; message/tool/identity not reflected safely in email.
